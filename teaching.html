Teaching Qualification

I hold the Baden-Württemberg Certificate for Teaching at University Level with a specialization in e-learning, a state-recognized program that combines pedagogical training, supervised teaching practice, and structured reflection. The certification has strengthened my foundation in evidence-based teaching and enables me to design courses with clear learning pathways, aligned learning objectives, and meaningful assessment formats.

My teaching experience spans Bachelor’s, Master’s, and professional education levels, including lectures, tutorials, seminars, and project-based courses taught in both English and German. I regularly supervise Bachelor’s and Master’s theses as well as interdisciplinary project teams, many of them in cooperation with industrial and societal partners, giving students opportunities to work on authentic and impactful real-world challenges.

My approach integrates structured guidance with space for student autonomy, combining hands-on work in Python and Jupyter Notebooks with a research-led perspective that encourages independent problem-solving and confident application of analytical methods.
